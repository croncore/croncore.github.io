What is Croncore?

Croncore is a full-stack AI research and software engineering firm serving global enterprises and US public-sector PRIMEs, delivering enterprise AI solutions, automation, agents, and speech AI. From research through deployment we own every layer of the stack, model, infrastructure, and product, so the systems we build hold up in production.

Who We Are

Who is Croncore and who does it build for?

Croncore LLC is a Delaware registered company founded in 2024, operating from four offices: Riyadh, Islamabad, London, and Dover, Delaware. It serves two kinds of buyer, global enterprises deploying AI into existing systems, and US public sector primes that need technical depth on SLED solicitations.

The work spans four engineering disciplines delivered by one team, and the same team carries a project from architecture through deployment rather than handing it between vendors.

Custom Software Development

Web and SaaS applications, enterprise application development, API development and integrations, and cloud native DevOps. See the discipline.

Cyber Security & Data

Security assessments and penetration testing, managed security and SOC, zero trust identity, and data engineering and analytics. See the discipline.

AI Agents & Automations

Enterprise AI agents, sales and CRM chatbots, operations automation, and process and document AI. See the discipline.

ERP & Enterprise Platforms

ERP implementation, Salesforce and ServiceNow, platform customization and extensions, and migrations and modernization. See the discipline.

Where We Are

Where is Croncore

Track Record

What has Croncore actually shipped?

Published work includes a sovereign neural voice engine for Mongolian telephony, an autonomous sales agent, a privacy first learning platform built from scratch as founding technical partner, and a custom voice agent closing the speed to lead gap, alongside pre-bid support on US municipal and state solicitations.

Sovereign Voice Engine for Mongolia

A self hosted neural synthesis platform for Bloomlink, built to remove per character cloud TTS billing and keep audio inside local infrastructure. Read the case study.

Autonomous Sales Agent

An agent that plans multi step work, calls APIs, writes to databases, and verifies the result, deployed at Bezninja. Read the case study.

Ground Up Digital Learning Platform

Founding technical partner to a Quebec EdTech startup, building a privacy first platform and sovereign AI tutor under Law 25 and GDPR. Read the case study.

SLED Pre-Bid Support

Technical and compliance support for US public sector primes across IT, cybersecurity, web development, and public works solicitations. See the SLED offering.
